# Angle: Blind — Black Box Contract

**Best for:** privacy threads, healthcare, journalism, security auditors  
**Hook:** We store it. We never read it. Not policy — architecture.

---

## One paragraph

`blind_payload` is encrypted client-side before ingest. No decryption key on server — engine cannot read content by design. **But:** `timestamp` and `location` are mandatory when blind is set. You may hide what's inside; you may not hide that you were here. For authorities: *Something was here. This time. This place.* What it was — unknown.

---

## Sound bite

> Protection by policy changes when companies sell. Protection by architecture doesn't.
