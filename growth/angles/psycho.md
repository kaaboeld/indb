# Angle: Psycho — Weight Ablation

**Best for:** HN Phase 3, Lobsters, DEV.to part 3, HABR, Twitter/X  
**Hook:** Every ranking carries your values. Psycho measures them instead of arguing about them.

---

## Problem (2 sentences)

Every ranking function smuggles the observer's weights — what matters, what costs, what to avoid. You can debate those weights forever. You almost never measure what they actually change on a real graph.
